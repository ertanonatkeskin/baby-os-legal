Baby OS Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

Baby OS ("Baby OS", "we", "us", or "our") is a baby care and parenting application designed to help parents and caregivers record, organize, understand, and review information related to a baby's daily care, development, health records, routines, and family coordination.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when it may be shared, and the choices available to you.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use Baby OS, we may process the following categories of information:

  • Account information, such as your email address and authentication information.
  • Baby profile information, such as name or nickname, date of birth, age-related information, and profile settings.
  • Care records, including sleep, feeding, diaper, routine, and related tracking information.
  • Health-related records that you choose to enter, such as temperature, medicine records, vaccination records, growth measurements, doctor visits, and parent-reported observations.
  • Development information, including parent-recorded observations, activities, and developmental history.
  • Family and caregiver information necessary to provide shared access and collaboration features.
  • Memories, notes, and photos that you intentionally upload or save.
  • Questions, requests, or messages that you intentionally send to Baby OS AI features.
  • Subscription and entitlement information.
  • Technical information such as device type, application version, operating system, crash information, and basic usage analytics.

3. How We Use Information

We may use your information to:

  • Provide baby tracking and care-record features.
  • Generate personalized summaries, patterns, predictions, and non-medical insights based on the records you enter.
  • Provide family and caregiver collaboration features.
  • Prepare parent-controlled summaries for doctor or professional visits.
  • Provide AI-assisted explanations and navigation where enabled.
  • Operate subscriptions and premium entitlements.
  • Maintain application security and prevent unauthorized access.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve application performance.
  • Understand general product usage through privacy-conscious analytics.

4. Health and Baby Information

Baby OS may process sensitive information about a child when you choose to enter that information. We treat this information as sensitive and design access controls around the baby and authorized family or caregiver roles.

Baby OS is not a medical provider and does not replace a pediatrician, physician, or other qualified healthcare professional.

Baby OS does not intend to diagnose medical conditions, prescribe treatment, or provide medication dosage recommendations.

5. AI Features

Some Baby OS features may use artificial intelligence to explain, summarize, or present information in a more useful way.

AI features are designed to operate on limited, relevant context and are not intended to act as a doctor or autonomous medical decision-maker.

Information may be transmitted to an AI service provider when you use an AI-powered feature. We aim to minimize the information sent and exclude unrelated private information where possible.

6. Service Providers

Baby OS may use third-party service providers to operate the application, including:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, storage, and backend infrastructure.
  • RevenueCat — subscription and entitlement management.
  • Anthropic — AI functionality where enabled.
  • PostHog — privacy-conscious product analytics where enabled.
  • Sentry — crash and error monitoring, using Sentry's European data region (see Section 9).
  • Expo / EAS — application build, update, and related infrastructure where applicable.
  • Apple — App Store distribution, payments, TestFlight, and platform services.

These providers may process limited information as necessary to provide their services and are subject to their own privacy and security terms.

7. Subscriptions and Payments

Payments for subscriptions purchased through the Apple App Store are processed by Apple. Baby OS does not receive your complete payment card information.

We may receive information about your subscription status, product, entitlement, renewal, or cancellation status in order to provide premium functionality.

8. Analytics

Baby OS may collect limited analytics data to understand how the product is used and to improve reliability and user experience.

We aim to avoid sending sensitive baby health content, private notes, medicine details, or other unnecessary personal content to analytics systems.

9. Crash and Error Monitoring

Baby OS uses Sentry to detect and diagnose crashes and unexpected errors. This information is used to investigate and fix technical problems, maintain the stability and security of the application, and debug issues that affect parents and caregivers.

Baby OS uses Sentry's European data region. Session replay is disabled, and storage of IP addresses and the collection of unnecessary personal information are disabled or filtered where supported by our configuration.

Crash and error reports may include technical metadata such as the application and build version, device and operating system information, stack traces and other error metadata, pseudonymous identifiers, and diagnostic or performance metadata.

Baby OS does not intentionally send baby names, health records, feeding or sleep logs, journal content, AI conversations, passwords, authentication tokens, or other sensitive care content to Sentry. Error reports are filtered before they are sent to reduce the chance that such information is included. Because error reports are generated automatically when something unexpected happens, we cannot guarantee that no personal information will ever be included, but we design and configure this processing to keep it to the minimum necessary.

10. Family and Caregiver Sharing

Baby OS may allow you to invite other people to access information associated with a baby.

Access depends on the permissions and role assigned to that person. You should only invite people you trust.

Authorized users may be able to view or add certain baby-care records depending on their assigned permissions.

11. Professional Sharing

Baby OS may allow parents to intentionally create and share limited reports with healthcare professionals or other trusted recipients.

Such sharing is initiated by the user and may use temporary or revocable access mechanisms where supported.

Saving a professional contact does not automatically provide that person with continuous access to the baby's account or records.

12. Photos and Memories

Photos, memories, and private notes are only processed when you choose to upload or create them.

Baby OS is designed to restrict access to private media and not make it publicly discoverable.

13. Data Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect user information, including authentication, role-based access controls, database security policies, restricted storage access, and encrypted network communication.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

14. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Baby OS, maintain your account, comply with applicable obligations, and protect the security of the service.

Certain temporary data, cached information, shared links, or technical logs may have shorter retention periods.

15. Account and Data Deletion

You may request or initiate deletion of your account and associated data through available Baby OS account controls or by contacting us.

Some limited records may need to be retained where required by law, security, fraud prevention, or legitimate operational requirements.

For deletion requests, contact: ertan.keskin@ivmenta.com

16. Children's Privacy

Baby OS is intended for parents, guardians, and adult caregivers. It is not designed for use directly by children.

Information about a child is entered and managed by an adult user.

17. International Processing

Where applicable, we aim to use appropriate contractual, technical, and organizational safeguards for such processing.
